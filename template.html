01 / Adversarial evaluation

A Periodic Table of Data Poisoning

Problem
Data-poisoning terminology and defenses are difficult to compare across attacks and settings.
My contribution
I co-developed the taxonomy and the public artifact that organizes attack, defense, and evaluation concepts.
Supported result
The repository exposes the taxonomy, experiment outputs, and a browser teaching experiment for inspection.
Limitation
The website does not rerun the paper experiments; its browser lab is an explicitly separate educational demonstration.
Code & taxonomy ↗Demo →Record ↓

02 / Auditable multimodal evidence

Matryoshka Authentication

Problem
Authentication claims can become opaque when 2D images, 3D geometry, and language-model evidence are combined.
My contribution
I co-authored an evidence framework that keeps these modalities and their provenance inspectable.
Supported result
The published article, dataset repository, notebooks, and analytic geometry demonstration are linked independently.
Limitation
The capsule geometry on this site illustrates the method; it is not an authenticity verdict or a reproduction of the full study.
Paper ↗Data ↗Demo ↓

03 / Computing education

The LLM Story

Problem
Transformer explanations often hide the relationship between visual metaphors and actual model state.
My contribution
I co-developed a synchronized visual narrative that connects token-, circuit-, and system-level views to measured or derived values.
Supported result
The accepted IEEE ISAIA 2026 record and public walkthrough make the educational artifact directly examinable.
Limitation
The visuals support explanation and preliminary diagnostics; they do not establish learning gains without a human study.
Demo ↗Publication record ↓

04 / Autonomous AI evaluation

testFAILS3

Problem
Manual model reviews become stale, while aggregate scores can hide missing coverage and provider failures.
My contribution
I developed a provenance-preserving evaluator that runs 14 versioned cases through configured model APIs and keeps official Arena context separate.
Supported result
The live dashboard and API expose suite and prompt hashes, assertion outcomes, latency, coverage, timestamps, and recorded errors. The saved run completed all 14 OpenAI cases; the Gemini failure remains visible and unscored.
Limitation
The cases are bounded regression probes, not a safety certification or human-preference study. Cross-model comparison requires matching suite versions and comparable coverage.

Research program

Test what AI does.

I study present-day AI systems through adversarial evaluation, multimodal verification, and inspectable educational tools. AGI and ASI risk remain a longer-term research direction, approached through explicit threat models and human oversight.

Featured research / AGI & ASI

AGI concerns broadly capable intelligence across tasks; ASI concerns intelligence exceeding human cognitive capabilities. My focus is preparation: evaluating capabilities and risks, strengthening safeguards, and educating people to question and verify what increasingly capable systems do.

Doctoral research / Rutgers University

As an NSF CSGrad4US Fellow, I work with Dov Kruger in Electrical and Computer Engineering, specializing in machine learning. My 2025–2026 work spans compression for AI training, distributed data-poisoning benchmarks, multimodal applications, and accessible STEM learning.

Explore the research domains

ASI risk & containment architecture

My research agenda connects AGI/ASI risk analysis with runtime monitoring, tool-authorization boundaries, and adversarial testing of prompt-injection defenses. The goal is to make the limits of autonomous systems measurable and their consequential actions reviewable.

AGI evaluationASI riskAI security

Work such as AutoNutriFact studies how claims can be checked against retrieved evidence, how tool actions can be constrained, and how validation decisions can be audited. These controls support verification; they do not prove that an agent cannot hallucinate.
