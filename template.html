01 / About

Where research meets
the classroom.

I’m Julie. My work brings together computing, education, and a long-standing interest in how complex systems behave.

I am a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science and Technology at Kean University, where I teach undergraduate and graduate courses and mentor student researchers. I am also pursuing a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, specializing in machine learning, at Rutgers University.

My background spans a Ph.D. in Finance, software development in laboratory and university settings, and more than a decade of teaching. Today, I focus on the security and trustworthiness of AI systems, multimodal learning, and making emerging technologies accessible to students.

Parametric torus

A surface built from two angles. Compare the triangle wireframe, interpolated vertex colors with and without an index buffer, and a checker texture.

02 / GRAPHICS STUDY

Swirl sphere

Latitude and longitude become a living color field. Change point density to reveal how sampling changes the surface, while the adapted swirl shader varies red light over time.

Download C++ / GLSL source

Red neon adaptation of the original spherical samples, grid mask and reveal shader. Original RGB formulas are preserved in the download.

03 / GRAPHICS STUDY

Sierpinski tetrahedron

A tetrahedron recursively divides into four smaller copies. Watch the structure subdivide and recombine, or inspect any depth with the slider. Level 4 contains 256 tetrahedra.

Download Processing source

Browser adaptation of the three acts in hw3_JK_pde.pde. Recursion is bounded for browser performance. The fern is a branching construction; the source identifies it as a work in progress, not a finished Barnsley IFS implementation.

04 / GRAPHICS STUDY

Koch snowflake

Each line segment becomes four shorter segments. Four iterations turn a triangle into a 768-segment snowflake boundary.

06 / GRAPHICS STUDY

Orbital systems

Follow Mercury, Venus, Earth and the Moon. The Earth–Moon hierarchy combines revolution with local motion; speed controls expose the different periods.

Download Processing source

Stylized browser adaptation of JK_HW2.pde. Uses the source periods and eccentric-orbit formulas with exaggerated object sizes and distances. Smooth materials replace the original image textures. This is a teaching model, not an ephemeris or a scale model.

07 / GRAPHICS STUDY

Collision fireworks

Neon balls rebound from the boundary. When two touch, both disappear into crimson particles. Restart the excerpt to replay the collision sequence.

Download full Processing game

Browser excerpt from JulieK_HW1.pde: ball collisions and particle bursts. This compact preview starts with 16 balls; the full source also includes gradual spawning, configuration and the steering Golden Snitch game.
