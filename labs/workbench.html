02 / Transparent cognitive pedagogy

Inside two attention blocks

Type up to 12 character tokens. Every Q, K, V projection and attention probability is recomputed in this browser. The fixed demonstration weights are untrained: this is an inspection tool, not a language model that understands or generates text.

The exact attention probabilities are available in the adjacent heatmap.

Bar height = attention probability (0–1). Columns: key positions; rows: query positions. The numerical heatmap is the authoritative readout.

Inspect Q, K, V matrices and the computation

Each block uses pre-layer normalization, one scaled dot-product attention head (QKᵀ / √8), causal softmax, an output projection and residual, then a 16-unit GELU feed-forward layer and residual. Character features and cosine position features are deterministic. Future tokens are masked. This small illustrative decoder differs from a trained GPT or OLMo checkpoint.

03 / 3D geometry & evidence

What a Blum medial axis actually represents

For this analytic capsule, the Blum medial axis is exactly the central line segment: the centers of its maximal inscribed balls. Move the amber ball to see how the skeleton and radius reconstruct the solid. Change the surface radius to measure a geometric discrepancy against the reference.

A capsule has a central medial-axis segment of length 2.20 units. Its maximal balls have the selected radius; the numeric measurements below remain available without WebGL.

This exact construction applies to a capsule, not to arbitrary point-cloud skeletonization. Geometric agreement alone does not establish an object's authenticity, origin, or evidential truth. Physical authentication needs independent material, provenance, and multimodal evidence.
